## Ingredients

- 7-8 medium-large Pink Lady, Gala, or Fuji apples (1.3-1.5kg / 3-3 1/2 pounds)
- Juice of 1/2 lemon
- 150g (3/4 cup) granulated sugar
- 55g (4 tablespoons) unsalted butter, cubed and kept very cold
- 1 sheet puff pastry, thawed but chilled
- Unsweetened whipped cream or creme fraiche, to serve (optional)

## Instructions

1. Heat oven to 205C/400F.
1. Peel the apples, cut into three large pieces around each core and remove any remaining seeds. Toss with the lemon juice.
1. Spread the sugar in a 28-30cm skillet and heat over medium-high without stirring for about 4 mins, until partly melted. Whisk until smooth, then reduce heat to medium-low.
1. Cook for another 1-2 mins until the caramel is dark amber. Remove from the heat and immediately whisk in the cold butter.
1. Add the apples and return to medium-high heat. Cook for about 10 mins, gently turning the pieces, until mostly tender with translucent edges but still holding their shape. Allow longer for larger or firmer apples. The caramel may harden at first but will loosen as the apples cook.
1. Transfer the apples to a separate 25cm ovenproof skillet or pie dish, rounded sides down. Arrange in a tight outer ring, overlapping each piece by about a third, then fill the centre. Leave out any pieces that will not fit.
1. Pour the remaining caramel over the apples and leave to cool for 10 mins.
1. Roll the pastry into a circle about 2.5cm wider than the dish, keeping it chilled until needed. Lay over the apples, tuck the edges inside the dish and cut one or two steam vents in the centre.
1. Place on a baking sheet and bake for 25-30 mins until the pastry is well browned and the caramel is bubbling around the edges.
1. Loosen the edge with a butter knife and leave to cool in the dish for 30-60 mins so the juices thicken. If completely cold, gently warm in the oven to loosen the caramel before turning out.
1. Cover with a serving plate and, holding the plate and dish firmly with oven gloves, carefully turn over. Lift off the dish and replace any apple pieces that have stuck.
1. Serve warm with whipped cream or creme fraiche. Refrigerate leftovers and reheat gently before serving.
